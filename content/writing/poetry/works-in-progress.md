---
title: Works in progress
date: 2025-01-28
---
We're all a work in progress,  
She told me today,  
It was our last session,  
And she sent me on my way.  

Keep chipping away at progress,  
Do a little at a time
Be kind to yourself,  
And mostly you'll be fine. 

Some days you'll feel all better,  
On top of everything,  
And some days rather wetter,  
As tears reach your chin.  

Life's always got some mess,  
That's the best and worst part,  
But keep chipping away at progress,   
With love in your heart.
