---
title: Ikigai
date: 2024-08-15
---

What makes you care,  
What drives you,  
To get from here,  
To there?  

What gets you up,  
What makes your day,  
What fills your cup,  
In every way.  

Do you know,  
What you need,  
To make your life,  
More than a need?  
To fill the moment,  
And the next,  
And never pause,  
For restful breath?  

How can you stop,  
How can you start,  
How will you dance,  
Before we part.  

The days are long but also short,  
What gets you,  
From here,  
To there.
