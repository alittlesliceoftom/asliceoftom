---
title: The Eclipse
date: 2026-08-12
---
Woe is me and all our days,  
As the sky fills up with glowing haze,  
Don't need no moon to hide suns rays,  
From our accumulated focused gaze.  


No the rage will do, or was it the clouds,  
Obscuring hearts, and angering crowds,  
This bitter storm, fills the weather chart,  
Leaves us with only, a total eclipse of the heart.
